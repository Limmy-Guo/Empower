# 🌟 Empower App

> **A social-health platform that turns lightweight profiles into actionable, real-world connections.**

[![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-MVP_Prototype-blue)]()
[![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Social_Health-green)]()

## 📖 Product Vision

In a world of digital noise, finding meaningful local connections is hard. **Empower** is designed to bridge the gap between digital intent and real-world wellbeing. 

Unlike traditional social networks that focus on content consumption, Empower focuses on **active participation**. By capturing user intent upfront ("What are you looking for?"), the app instantly matches members with people, organizations, and activities that fit their specific growth and wellness goals.

## 📱 Key Features & User Flow

### 1. ⚡ Streamlined Onboarding (Registration)
A low-friction entry point designed to capture essential data for the recommendation algorithm without overwhelming the user.
* **Dynamic Interest Tags:** Users can add/remove up to three core interests to signal current priorities.
* **Intent Selector:** A strategic "What are you looking for?" toggle (Organizations vs. Activities vs. Connections) to tailor the downstream experience.
* **Location-Based:** Ensures all matches are actionable and local.

### 2. 👤 Holistic Member Profile
The digital identity card for the user, designed for quick scanning and storytelling.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Clean header with avatar and role tags.
* **Interest Chips:** Pill-style visual indicators for shared interests.
* **Persistent Navigation:** A bottom navigation bar (Home, Events, Chats, Recommendations, Profile) anchors the user experience, allowing seamless switching between discovery and personal space.

### 3. 🔍 Intelligent Discovery Engine (Recommendations)
The core value proposition of the app. Instead of a chaotic feed, results are structured into four curated buckets to match user goals:
* **🤝 Friendship Matches:** Peer-to-peer connections based on shared profiles.
* **d🏘️ Community Activities:** Local group events and gatherings.
* **🚀 Skill & Growth:** Opportunities for professional or personal development.
* **🌿 Wellness & Lifestyle:** Activities focused on health and mental wellbeing.

### 4. 🎛️ Dynamic Filtering
* Users retain control with a dedicated **Filter Page**, allowing them to refine recommendations by event type, time, or specific categories if the automated suggestions don't meet immediate needs.

## 🛠️ Design Philosophy

* **Action-Oriented:** Every screen pushes the user toward a real-world interaction (joining an event, messaging a peer).
* **Simplicity:** Minimalist UI with clear CTAs (Call to Actions) to reduce cognitive load.
* **Wellness-First:** The "Social-Health" focus ensures the algorithm prioritizes positive, growth-oriented connections over viral content.
